# Design Philosophy — Reading Almanac

Reading Almanac makes a complex chart feel like a calm reference book.

## Principles

1. **Explain before decorating.** Every section should answer one visitor question.
2. **Use editorial restraint.** Scale, spacing, rules, and typography create hierarchy; chrome stays quiet.
3. **Make the bodygraph the evidence.** The chart is the dominant visual and every gate/channel remains readable.
4. **Keep actions literal.** “Generate your chart,” “Start over,” and “Unlock the full reading” state exactly what happens.
5. **Reflow, never squeeze.** Mobile layouts stack in reading order without horizontal overflow.
6. **Preserve trust.** Birth data, payment state, and locked content receive explicit status and no decorative ambiguity.

## Avoid

- Mystical visual clichés, cosmic gradients, or ornamental zodiac imagery.
- Dashboard-card mosaics and competing calls to action.
- More than one primary accent or more than two principal typefaces.
- Tiny chart labels, detached channel lines, or color-only chart meaning.
- Animation that delays chart reading or interferes with form completion.

## Adaptation

Customer copy, imagery, offer details, and accent color may change through AstroPages. Preserve the editorial hierarchy, readable chart geometry, interaction states, responsive behavior, and platform contracts when adapting the template.
